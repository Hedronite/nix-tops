### Added

- The kit gates itself: a root `nix-tops.toml` (`hard`, facets `package-set` and `dev-flake`) with the five layers that are not built switched off with the reason "not built in M1", and `.github/workflows/nix-tops.yml` with the `gate`, `determinate` and `law-diff` jobs.
- `scripts/layers_ran.py`: after the gate, fails the job if a layer is missing from the report, an enabled layer did not run, or a switched-off layer does not show `skip` with its reason.
- `scripts/law_diff.py`: the `law-diff` job runs the base commit copy of this script on the change files, read as data. It fails a weakening without the `protocol-change` label, including a new, changed or later-dated `[[warnings.allow]]` entry, and passes as vacuous while the base has no script. A change under `NIX_TOPS.md`, `schema/`, `laws/`, `profiles/`, `.github/workflows/` or either script needs the label as well.
