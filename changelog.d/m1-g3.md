### Added

- `nixtops outputs [<flake>] [--json]`: every output of a flake at every system with its `.drv` path, name, `meta` (description, homepage, license, main program) and the error evaluating it gave. The walk uses the kit's pinned CppNix and `nix-eval-jobs` under gate 3's options (warnings fatal, import from derivation off, never `--impure`) and does not stop at the first error. Apps, overlays, modules and templates are instantiated at the levels of §6.1; configurations give their build root; `legacyPackages`, `lib` and unknown outputs are listed. Coverage is `not-evaluated`. The command exits 0 when the inventory is complete, errors included.
- `src/inventory` exposes the derived facet set (`derived_facets`) and the exported systems (`exported_systems`) of a flake's structure, for the config check.
- `docs/evidence/2026-11-18-inventory/`: the command over `python-gate` at `2cb1775` (13 outputs, 0 errors) and `rust-tops` at `77056c9` (every `x86_64-darwin` failure in one run), on a Determinate daemon and a CppNix daemon with identical results.
