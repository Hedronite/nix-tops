### Changed

- `scripts/spec_check.py catalog` now compares each `laws/N-NN.toml` with its section 8.2 row, field by field: `id`, `law` (markdown emphasis stripped, whitespace normalized), `severity`, `reader`, `reader_also`, `reader_detail`, `sketch`, `artifacts` and `judged`. A mismatch fails and names the ID, the field, the file's value and the row's value. `scripts/tests/test_spec_check.py` plants one flip per field.
- `laws/README.md` states exactly what is compared and what is not (`title`, `rationale`, `fix`, `option_names`). It no longer says the comparison belongs to the runner.
