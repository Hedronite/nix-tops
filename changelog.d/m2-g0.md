### Added

- `lib.mkChecks` in `nix/lib/` (`nix/lib/default.nix`, `nix/lib/mkChecks.nix`, `nix/lib/parse_findings.py`), exported as the kit's `lib` output and merged into the kit's own `checks` in `flake.nix`. Emits `nixtops-<tool>-findings` (writes `{ tool, version, config_hash, files }` JSON to `$out` and fails closed on tool, config, or output errors) and `nixtops-<tool>` (reads findings and fails on any finding) for `nixfmt` (via `treefmt --ci`), `statix`, `deadnix`, and `gitleaks` on the first supported system in `systems` (no IFD: `builtins.fromTOML`). Filters `.nix` files and tool configs for the three Nix linters (`nixfmt`, `statix`, `deadnix`) so unrelated edits (such as `README.md`) keep all six Nix-linter `drvPath`s unchanged; `gitleaks` scans the tracked tree.
- Gate 2 (`crates/nixtops/src/gate/lint.rs`) and `schema/gate-report.schema.json` optional `lint` field: `nixtops gate` builds the findings derivations on the host system (using `checks.<host>`, `passthru.forSystem`, or the embedded `nix/lib` fallback), records tool versions, config hashes, and per-file finding counts sorted by tool name in `.nix-tops/report.json`, honors `lint.deadnix.fail`, and fails under `hard` when any failing finding exists. `nixtops-lint` waits for M3 (`NIX_TOPS.md` §1.4 and §3.1 gate 2 row updated).
- Laid out the `src/baseline/` seam (`src/lib.rs`, `src/baseline/mod.rs`), `src/gate/mod.rs`, `src/gate/run.rs`, and `src/report/` dispatch for lint, build-test, and ratchet.
- Gate 2 fixtures `fixtures/gates/lint-clean`, `fixtures/gates/lint-deadnix`, and `fixtures/gates/lint-statix`, plus `crates/nixtops/tests/lint_run.rs` and `crates/nixtops/tests/lint_fixtures.rs`.
